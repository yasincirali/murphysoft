Kepler Drift

One-finger orbit arcade. Hold to circle a planet, let go to launch, land softly.

Hold the planet, then let go.

How Kepler Drift plays

  • Hold to orbit. The probe locks onto the nearest planet and circles it. Let go and it leaves on a straight line.
  • Tap to burn. A little fuel corrects the course in flight.
  • Arrive slowly. Reach the target under the speed limit. Extra orbits are your brakes.
Status
Early build. These screens come from the playable prototype.
Missions
Each solar system is a chapter of 12 missions.
