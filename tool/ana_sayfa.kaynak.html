What happens when it goes wrong

Murphy’s law says anything that can go wrong will. Here are six cases our apps are built to handle.

What goes wrongAppWhat happens next
A price source stops respondingSandıkThe point is left off the chart. No number is ever made up.
Someone signs in on another phoneSandıkA code goes to your email and the old session closes.
A request fails halfway through a tapSandıkYou see the reason in plain words and can try again.
You want to leaveSandıkDownload your data or delete the account from Settings.
You don’t want another accountBirdie SmashNo account needed. Your progress stays with you.
A child is holding the phoneMy Pocket MoneyIt shows no ads and tracks nothing. All data stays on the device.
