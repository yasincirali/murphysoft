# My Pocket Money Support

Write to **support@murphysoft.app**. We read every message and usually reply within two days. It helps to include the phone model and its system version.

My Pocket Money is called Harçlıklarım in Turkish. It is made for children aged 7–15 and set up by a parent.

## We are changing phones

All data stays on the phone; we have no server and no account. If the phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is turned on, the app's data is part of that backup and comes back when the new phone is restored from it.

## The gold or currency character shows no value

Today's value comes from a public market data provider and needs an internet connection. The request carries nothing about the child or the parent. Without a connection the app does not show an invented number.

## Sending feedback from the app

In Parents' corner, after the parent PIN, tap **Send feedback**. The text is shown first and contains no names or amounts. You send it with an app you choose, such as email.

## Starting over

In Parents' corner, **Delete all data** removes the profile, feedings and stickers from the phone. This cannot be undone. Deleting the app does the same.

## Privacy

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no in-app purchases. Read the [privacy policy](/pocket-money/privacy/).
