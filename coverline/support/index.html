I am changing phones

Everything you add stays on your phone, so move it with a backup file. Open Settings › Backup › Back up the cabinet. The file holds every product, photo and receipt. Save it to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone, open the same screen and tap Restore from a backup. Restoring replaces what is on that phone.

The backup file is a plain zip and is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

A reminder did not arrive

Reminders before a protection ends come from your phone's own notification system. Check that notifications are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

The app says a country is "Not verified yet"

We only state legal rights for countries whose rules we have checked against official sources. For other countries the app shows the manufacturer warranty and points you to the official consumer authority. The rights shown are general information, not legal advice.

I want a list of everything I own

Settings › Export makes a PDF report (to print or keep) or an Excel spreadsheet (to sort and filter). The file is created on your phone and you choose where it goes.
